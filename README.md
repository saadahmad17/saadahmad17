## 💼 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer with **8+ years of professional experience**, currently pursuing a **PhD in Computer Science at UET Lahore**. My research sits at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation**, with a focus on low-resource South Asian languages.

- 🔭 Currently working on **RAG-based applications, chatbots, and LLM fine-tuning**
- 🌍 Special interest in **Urdu NLP** and underserved South Asian languages
- 📄 Published at **CHiPSAL 2026 (LREC)** — *DR-RAG: Addressing Retrieval Misalignment in Low-Resource Urdu Question Answering*
- 🤝 Looking to collaborate on **Generative AI and NLP research**
