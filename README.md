## 💼 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer with **8+ years of professional experience**, currently pursuing a **PhD in Computer Science at UET Lahore**. My research sits at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation**, with a particular focus on low-resource South Asian languages — especially Urdu.

- 🔭 Currently **researching and building** RAG-based applications, intelligent chatbots, and fine-tuned LLM pipelines
- 🌍 Deep focus on **Urdu NLP** and closing the performance gap for underserved South Asian languages
- 📄 **Published at CHiPSAL 2026 (LREC)** — *DR-RAG: Addressing Retrieval Misalignment in Low-Resource Urdu Question Answering*
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on **Generative AI, NLP research, and low-resource language systems**
- 💡 Bridging the gap between **production-grade software engineering** and **cutting-edge AI research**
